# Patient User Guide - Synapse AI

Welcome to Synapse AI, your intelligent hospital management system. This guide will help you navigate the patient portal.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Registration
1. Visit the patient intake page
2. Select your preferred language from the dropdown (23 Indian languages supported)
3. Enter your personal details:
   - Full name
   - Age
   - Phone number
   - Sex
4. Provide your vitals:
   - Blood pressure (systolic/diastolic, e.g., 120/80)
   - Blood sugar (mg/dL)
   - Temperature (°C)
   - Weight (kg)
   - Height (cm)
5. Click "Register" to create your account

### Step 2: Symptom Description
After registration, you'll speak with an AI assistant to describe your symptoms:
- You can type your symptoms or use voice input
- The AI will ask follow-up questions in your selected language
- Be honest and detailed about your symptoms
- The conversation helps doctors understand your condition better

### Step 3: Upload Medical Reports (Optional)
If you have recent medical reports:
1. Click "Upload Report" button
2. Select a file (PDF, JPG, PNG, or WebP)
3. The AI will analyze the report and extract key findings
4. You'll see color-coded results:
   - 🟢 Green: Normal values
   - 🟡 Yellow: Slightly abnormal
   - 🔴 Red: Severely abnormal
5. Each parameter shows:
   - Your value
   - Normal range
   - AI-generated impact explanation
   - Suggestions in your language

### Step 4: Compare Reports (Optional)
If you have multiple reports:
1. Click "Compare Reports" button
2. Select two reports (past and present)
3. View side-by-side comparison
4. See improvement indicators:
   - ↑ Improved
   - ↓ Deteriorated
   - → Unchanged
5. Read AI-generated summary in your language

### Step 5: Get Your Token
After completing intake:
1. Click "Get Token" to join the queue
2. You'll receive:
   - Your token number (e.g., S-260413-1)
   - Assigned doctor's name and specialty
   - Estimated wait time
   - Your priority level (low/medium/high)
3. The system automatically assigns the right doctor based on your symptoms

### Step 6: Visit Dashboard
Access your patient dashboard to:
- View your assigned doctor
- Check your queue position
- See estimated wait time
- View your visit history
- Access your reports and prescriptions

## What to Expect

### During Intake
- AI assistant guides you through the process
- All conversations saved for your doctor
- Takes about 5-10 minutes
- You can pause and resume

### After Registration
- Receive your queue token
- Wait in designated area
- Check dashboard for updates
- Doctor will call your token

### During Consultation
- Doctor reviews your AI-generated summary
- Discusses your symptoms and reports
- May prescribe medications
- Prescriptions saved to your dashboard

### After Consultation
- View prescriptions in dashboard
- Download or print prescriptions
- Access visit history anytime
- Upload follow-up reports

## Tips for Best Experience

1. **Be Honest**: Accurate information helps doctors help you better
2. **Upload Reports**: Recent reports help AI provide better insights
3. **Use Voice**: Speech recognition works well in Indian languages
4. **Check Dashboard**: Stay updated on your queue status
5. **Compare Reports**: Track your health over time

## Troubleshooting

### Language Not Working
- Ensure you selected the correct language
- Try typing instead of voice
- Clear browser cache and try again

### Report Upload Failed
- Check file size (max 5MB)
- Ensure file is PDF, JPG, PNG, or WebP
- Try a different file

### Can't See Token
- Ensure you completed registration
- Check if you clicked "Get Token"
- Refresh the page

### AI Not Responding
- Check internet connection
- Try again after a few seconds
- Use fallback text input
